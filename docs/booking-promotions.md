# Promotion codes during online booking

A customer can enter an optional code in **Review and confirm**, then select **Review offer**. The server prices the selected services for that account, professional and appointment start. The review shows the additional offer saving, the booking total, existing membership savings when applicable, and any local weekday/start/lead restrictions. Entering a code requires a successful current review or explicit removal before confirmation.

The price review binds the customer, ordered service quote identities, professional, start instant, promotion revision, timezone and calculated amounts. It reserves neither time nor quota. Confirmation checks all of them again under the existing scheduling/calendar/customer coordination, locks the code's usage and writes the appointment, original service snapshots, service-only receipt, reward and deposit policy in one transaction. A failed check leaves no partial visit, receipt, quota claim or intake response. Competing customers cannot both reserve the final use of a code.

The existing membership discount and the promotion do not stack their full percentages: each line keeps its better saving. The review distinguishes existing benefits from the additional offer. Gift/pass purchase value, retail items and tips are not added to the booking price. The original treatment names/prices remain visible, with a separate booking discount and the agreed total in confirmation and customer appointment cards.

When deposits are enabled, changing the reviewed offer invalidates the customer's policy acceptance, including when only its revision changes. The displayed deposit is calculated on the discounted booking total. A 100 EUR visit, 20% offer and 25% deposit policy require a 20 EUR deposit against an 80 EUR visit. The confirmation request binds that net amount and the policy revision. A fully discounted visit has no fictitious payment or deposit.

Original online promotion receipts use the stable server-generated `booking-promotion-<appointment UUID>` creation key. Their service benefits, reward fulfillment and commissions wait for appointment completion, including when deposits are disabled. The deferred zero-total settlement phase first installs the visit's deposit policy, then evaluates fulfillment. Cancelling an unperformed visit releases its coupon and closes the receipt. Captured funds remain in the original payment/refund ledger; cancellation never pretends that a refund was delivered. Existing financial refund and reconciliation procedures still apply.

An edited, archived, exhausted or newly ineligible offer requires a new review or explicit removal, preserving the selection and confirmed private form answers. The booking is not silently repriced without the offer. Saved timing conditions continue to govern rescheduling, independently of subsequent offer changes. Repeating a successful original request recovers its original visit and receipt even after the source offer is archived.

## Uncertain requests

While sending, the reviewed packet and its intent UUID freeze. If the response is lost, malformed or uncertain, the same packet is retained in memory and fields remain disabled. Confirm again to recover that same result. A later authentication or other rejection cannot prove that the original request failed; it does not unlock editing. Navigation warns that the visit may already exist. **Check my appointments** provides an explicit recovery path before making another reservation. Nothing about this packet or private form answers is written to browser storage. A late response cannot redirect a different page opened meanwhile.

## Scope and operation

This online review is for an authenticated customer booking their own individual visit. Team-created appointments can use the existing explicit unpaid-receipt promotion workflow. Series requests reject the new individual promotion packet: each visit needs its own eligible, quota-aware review. There is no automatic campaign delivery or bank activation. Configuring an actual deposit still requires an enabled payment provider and its real credentials; local verification uses the isolated provider fixture.

No additional database migration is needed beyond the existing receipt/reward data and timing snapshot from `0039`. Clients may omit the new promotion field, preserving legacy booking identities and payloads. The optional `bookingPrice` response reports the original online booking service totals; standalone sales, retail and tips cannot inflate that visit amount. Full service snapshots remain immutable.

Focused regression coverage checks scoped/stale reviews, atomic quota races, discounted deposits, original-request recovery after source archive, conserved membership savings, free visits, advance payment, cancellation and real refund-ledger outcomes. Browser verification covers changed-offer review, renewed policy acceptance, lost post-commit response, subsequent authentication error, navigation recovery, the original receipt/visit identity, discounted confirmation, Spanish, accessibility and overflow. These are synthetic local accounts, prices and transactions.
