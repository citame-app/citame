# Booking deposits and cancellation

The booking policy is disabled by default. An owner can configure it under Settings: percentage (up to 100%) or fixed EUR deposit capped at the service bill, a 5–60 minute payment window, free-cancellation notice, late/no-show percentages and optional trusted-customer exemption. Active services must use EUR. Configure and verify the real online payment terminal before requiring customer deposits. No customer/card data or terminal credentials are invented by setup.

Each new protected booking freezes the accepted policy revision, terms, customer, consent time and server-priced receipt. Customer acceptance includes the displayed service total; a changed price requires another review. Existing eligible membership discounts reduce the bill and deposit. The deposit is a partial payment of the same receipt used for the visit, rather than a second service charge. The unpaid service remainder becomes payable after the appointment is completed. Service revenue and commissions are not earned until completion, even when a 100% deposit has already been paid.

The customer sees a payment deadline and the cancellation policy before confirmation. A held time is not described as confirmed until the required deposit is funded. Confirmation/reminder jobs are scheduled only after funding (or an exemption); expiry withdraws jobs and releases the appointment. The independent API expiry loop runs every minute, processing up to 100 holds per cycle. Its receipt locks make concurrent replicas safe. Bank callbacks also enforce expiry immediately, even if the scheduled expiry loop has not yet run. A late capture is refundable reconciliation and cannot restore the appointment or take someone else's time.

Owners/managers may explicitly waive a deposit when creating a salon booking; enter a reason of at least eight characters. The server verifies the account's current permissions and records the reason privately in the audit trail. Other staff cannot manufacture customer consent or bypass the rule through REST, MCP or offline replay. Trusted customers can be exempt when enabled in the policy. Prepaid visit redemptions require a salon waiver before booking, because visits must be applied before monetary tender; a deposit and a prepaid visit cannot independently pay the same service line.

Cancelling before the recorded cutoff makes the entire captured deposit due back. Late cancellation/no-show retains the configured percentage of the discounted service bill, capped by the deposit actually captured and still retained. No extra automatic card charge is made. Expiry has no fee. A receipt closed for cancellation/no-show/expiry preserves its original price and payments, shows zero payable balance and records the retained fee and remaining refund due. Pending bank attempts may still settle and must be returned through the original-payment reconciliation/refund workflow.

Refunds remain an explicit salon operation from the receipt, subject to refund permissions. An online refund is dispatched through Redsys only after the manager requests and executes it; unknown bank outcomes require reconciliation and are never retried blindly. The customer sees what is still due back. Refunding some/all of a retained fee writes a negative fee movement. Reports/CSV show the net fee recognized within the selected period separately from delivered-service sales and collected cash. Managers can waive a fee by refunding it; the original receipt is not rewritten. Returning enough of a required deposit to leave it underfunded cancels the held/confirmed reservation without a penalty and withdraws its reminders. If its deadline has elapsed, it is recorded as expired. The remaining captured balance remains due back. Requested bank refunds remain due back until the bank confirms success.

Rescheduling preserves the payment deadline and freezes the accepted policy. Before the free-cancellation deadline, the cutoff follows the new appointment time. A move after that deadline is rejected to prevent moving into the future and then cancelling for free; the salon can cancel, review/refund the deposit and create a replacement booking. Closed protected appointments cannot be reopened through status correction. Their financial record and released time remain intact.

No external credential is needed for the disabled state. `bun run --cwd apps/web e2e:deposits` starts an isolated local database/API and an explicit simulated gateway; it intercepts the hosted-bank destination before any external request. The fixture executable is never imported by production.
