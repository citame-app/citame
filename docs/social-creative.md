# Local social creative

Owners and managers use **Reputation → Social creative** to prepare an image and its complete caption from a current bookable treatment, a service offer or a published review with separate social permission. This is for the single Sisters of Beauty salon. It does not connect to or publish on a social account.

Choose a source, square (1080×1080), portrait (1080×1350) or story (1080×1920), and one of three colour palettes. Prepare and inspect the preview, including the complete caption and permission, then confirm the review before downloading a PNG or text caption, or copying the caption. Images render locally with bundled fonts and a locally generated booking QR. Booking links use the current site's origin; configure the real production domain before distributing real material. Private client photos are never imported into this composer.

Treatment images show the current price, currency and duration. Offers require an active eligible EUR service promotion that is neither expired nor exhausted. They show the code, eligible treatment, minimum spend, discount cap, per-client and total limits, start/end dates in the salon's time zone, and exclusions. A future start is explicit. Advertised discounts say “up to”; checkout eligibility and current availability determine the actual discount. Complete conditions are never truncated: if they cannot fit at a readable size, preparation fails and asks for a larger format. Treatment descriptions may be shortened in the image; the caption retains the full description.

A review's website publication agreement does not permit social reuse. Clients must opt in separately; the control is initially unchecked, and editing wording requires a new choice. Published legacy reviews have no social permission. The operator sees only currently published, eligible, consented reviews. A review image can include a clearly labelled excerpt and the client-chosen public name/rating; the caption retains the full original wording. It never copies the client's private identity or appointment ID. Do not manually add private information to the downloaded artwork.

Clients can withdraw social permission while retaining the website review. Full withdrawal, an edit, unpublication, an ineligible corrected appointment outcome or deletion of the client profile also prevents new exports. Each preview and each export fetches its current source with HTTP cache bypass. Changed terms or review revisions invalidate the reviewed preview, preserve the selections and require reloading and preparation again. Already downloaded or copied files cannot be recalled; the withdrawal dialog explains this limitation. The permission version is `public-review-social-v1`; audit metadata records permission/state/version, never the feedback or public name. Original submission fingerprints stay compatible with website-only retries.

Draft images and review selections stay in memory, not browser storage. Network and clipboard failures are reported without publishing anything. There are no external image generation or social network requests. Actual public review permissions and business offer terms must be supplied by their owners; fixtures are synthetic.

Verification covers strict contracts, independent consent, version conflicts, account/role boundaries, completed-visit eligibility, original retry compatibility, withdrawal, text fit and Unicode graphemes, changed price rejection and fresh-source requests. Browser workflows exercise PNG dimensions and independent QR decoding, full offer terms, overflow recovery, client opt-in, moderation, Spanish withdrawal, light/dark mobile/desktop, axe and horizontal overflow.
